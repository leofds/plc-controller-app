Introduction
PLC Controller ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

1. Information We Do Not Collect
   We do not collect, store, or share any personal or sensitive information from our users. The App operates entirely locally on your device, and no data is transmitted to us or any third parties.

2. Local Data Storage
   All data generated or used by the App is stored locally on your device. This includes any preferences, settings, or credentials you configure within the App. We do not have access to this data, and it remains under your control at all times.

3. Amazon AWS IoT Access
   The App allows you to connect to Amazon AWS IoT by manually entering your credentials. These credentials are stored locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences system. We do not transmit, store, or have access to your AWS credentials or any data you interact with on AWS.

4. Permissions
   The App does not require any special permissions to access your device's data, such as contacts, location, or media. It operates with minimal permissions necessary for its functionality.

5. Data Security
   Since all data is stored locally on your device, its security depends on your device's settings and protections. We recommend that you keep your device secure and avoid sharing it with unauthorized individuals.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
   We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
